# d-class-amp

This electronic printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) is used in classes during electronics education. It is part of a Class-D amplifier.

## Educational Context

In the Electronics Engineering degree program, students complete the laboratory course **"PREL"** across the entire 3rd semester.
During this course, the hardware is developed and integrated step by step:

1. **Breadboard Prototype:** Students build a discrete Class-D amplifier on a breadboard, consisting of a microphone/input preamplifier, a tone control stage, a triangle wave generator, and a comparator.
2. **PCBA Integration:** This integrated printed circuit board assembly is then mounted onto the breadboard to replace or complement the discrete stages.
3. **Output Filter Design:** Finally, students dimension and assemble a 4-pole (4th-order) output low-pass filter on the breadboard before connecting the loudspeaker.
